# Local audiovisual playback samples

`demo-origen.mp4`, `demo-pulse.mp4`, `demo-nocturna.mp4`, `demo-materia.mp4` and
`demo-horizonte.mp4` are fictional local-preview assets, created on 2026-10-03.
Each is 8 seconds long at 960 × 540, 24 fps, with H.264 video and stereo AAC audio.

These are **animated AI covers with synthesized audio**, intended to demonstrate
actual browser video playback and the hub frame. They are not full generated
films, client work, commercial deliverables or claimed portfolio results. The
on-video caption identifies them as playback samples. The concept covers are
the corresponding `/img/demo-*.webp` assets.

Framing motion, graphic captions and sound were generated locally. FFmpeg
encoded the files; its tool binary is temporary and is not included in the site.
Only the isolated preview fixture adds the five DEMO projects. Production data
is not seeded or modified by these assets.
